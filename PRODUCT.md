# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Everyday Indian households, homemakers, budget-conscious families, festive shoppers, and residents in and around Jaipur looking for high-utility kitchenware, organizers, home decor, pooja essentials, and personal care without paying retail middleman markups.

## Product Purpose

Griham 99+ ("Shop More. Save More.") provides a royal, modern, and accessible retail storefront showcasing 99+ essential home & living items starting at just ₹49. It bridges the gap between local brick-and-mortar convenience (Tonk Phatak, Jaipur) and seamless online shopping with Cash on Delivery (COD), UPI, and fast local dispatch.

## Positioning

Authentic Indian domestic utility and handcrafted festive decor under ₹99, sourced directly from verified manufacturers. Unlike generic drop-shipping sites or premium luxury lifestyle brands, Griham 99+ balances cultural trust (Jaipur heritage, Hindi/English bilingual UI, WhatsApp order assistance, local physical store in Tonk Phatak) with transparent, ultra-accessible pricing starting at ₹49.

## Operating Context

- Mobile-first Indian e-commerce landscape: High mobile traffic (often 4G/slow networks, UPI payments, WhatsApp communication, Cash on Delivery expectation).
- Physical retail hub at Gali No. 15, Sachivalaya Colony, Adarsh Bazar, Barkat Nagar, Tonk Phatak, Jaipur (302015).
- Peak shopping during festive seasons (Diwali, Navratri, weddings) and daily domestic replenishment (kitchen tools, organizers).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 14.2 App Router with React 18, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, and MongoDB Atlas data layer.
- Cloudinary media delivery with automatic image bounding and format optimization.
- Client state synchronized with localStorage for Cart and Wishlist with instant drawer slide-outs.
- Strict security constraints: Server-side anti-tamper order total calculation, Zod validation, NoSQL injection protection, rate limiting, and timing-safe admin authentication.
- Bilingual English and Hindi interface toggle.
- Pincode delivery serviceability checker.

## Product Principles

1. **Honest Domestic Value**: Every item must solve a real daily household need or festive tradition at authentic Indian retail prices (₹49, ₹79, ₹89, ₹99+).
2. **Instant Cultural Trust**: Clear bilingual communication, Jaipur heritage context, transparent pricing, and zero hidden shipping surprises.
3. **Frictionless Mobile Utility**: Fast load times, quick-view inspection, one-tap WhatsApp support, and seamless cart/checkout flow.
4. **Refined Craftsmanship over Generic Gimmicks**: Avoid tech-bro aesthetics or irrelevant imagery; showcase genuine Indian home products and craftsmanship.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Full bilingual screen reader semantics and aria labels on all interactive controls.
- Keyboard accessible dialogs, search auto-suggest, and drawer navigation with focus trapping.
- Reduced motion preferences honored for sensitive users.
